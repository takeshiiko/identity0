## Intent

Launch promo for Tyr ("liquidity daemon", beta) — https://www.tyrliquidity.app/.
Tyr opens a Meteora (Solana) or Uniswap v3/v4 liquidity position in one pass:
paste a token address and a budget → scan → buy → plan → sign. The desk builds
transactions for the connected wallet and never holds a key; nothing is signed
until the user confirms. Audience: DeFi traders / LPs on X. Sell, not tour.
