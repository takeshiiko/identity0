## Intent

Second cut of the Tyr promo (v1: `../tyr-promo`). Put the desk's three moves up front:
fast open (paste → run → execute, one pass through scan / buy / plan / sign / confirm),
rebalance (price left the band → close and re-open around the new price, same pool, fee and band),
compound (claim the fees and put them straight back in). Different look from v1's dark terminal:
a light editorial poster — bone paper, black ink, lime highlighter, runes as section marks.
