## Intent

v3 of the Tyr promo, modelled on the user's reference SaaS launch film (continuous camera, live UI,
motion blur, depth, SFX). Keeps v2's light editorial look (the user's pick): bone paper, ink slabs,
lime highlighter, runes. One world: six boards (problem, brand, venues, open, rebalance, compound)
that the camera travels between, then pulls back to show the whole desk before the CTA.

## Customizations

- UI rebuilt as live HTML (ticket, pipeline, position band chart, fee tiles) instead of screenshots.
- Ambient music bed + synced SFX (whoosh / click / typing / pop / chime). Placeholder music until a licensed track arrives;
  `assets/audio/sfx.mp3` is the SFX layer alone, to remix over a new track.
